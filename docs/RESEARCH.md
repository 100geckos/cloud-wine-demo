## Owner handoff checklist

- Confirm business/display name, address including suite/postcode, phone, email, social accounts, and full current weekly/holiday hours.
- Supply approved logo, colors, owned/licensed photos, image permissions, and short business story.
- Supply a current menu with prices, descriptions, allergens, availability, and approval date.
- Supply event dates, start/end times, timezone, announcement URLs, and any booking rules.
- Confirm required accessibility accommodations, reservation/contact process, and privacy requirements before adding data collection.
- Decide preview authorization, hosting account, domain ownership, editor access, and final launch approval.
